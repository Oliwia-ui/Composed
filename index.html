03 · inside my head

I don't always have a quiet mind. But I can create something clear from it.

keep scrolling. it starts making sense.

  1. 01
    make it make sense.I don't like things that are complicated just because they can be. I ask questions, pull things apart and try again until the whole thing feels right. Not just to me. To the person using it.
  2. 02
    people come first.I think about the person on the other side of the screen. Their time, their feelings, their bad days and the things they might not know how to explain. Good design should make their life a little easier, not give them another thing to figure out.
  3. 03
    pretty isn’t enough.I love a good interface. But what happens when someone clicks it? Does it work? Does it make sense? Can I build it? I want to understand the whole thing, not just the part that looks good in a screenshot.
