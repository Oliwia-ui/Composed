about me

usually somewhere between figma and vs code

my head is busy. my work needs to make sense.

I'm Oliwia, a digital design student at Thomas More, currently looking for a design internship. I like understanding how things work, why people behave the way they do and how a good idea can become something people actually use.

I'm a designer with a developer's curiosity. I enjoy the whole journey: asking questions, doing research, figuring out the structure, designing the screens and getting my hands into the code. I can spend far too long on one small detail, but I want that detail to have a reason.

My brain rarely works in a straight line. I question things, change direction, come back to old ideas and occasionally make things harder for myself than they need to be. But I like that about the way I work. I don't just accept the first answer. I want to understand it.

I don't design just to make things look good. I want to know who needs it, why they need it and whether it actually helps.

usually somewhere underwater

above water, a thousand thoughts. underwater, one breath at a time.

When I'm not studying or working on something, there's a good chance I'm thinking about my next dive. I started diving even though I was genuinely scared of it. The deeper I got into it, the more I realised how much I loved being underwater.

There's something about diving that makes you slow down. You check your equipment, pay attention to your surroundings and control your breathing. You can't rush everything. You have to stay present and trust what you've learned.

I love exploring new places, seeing things I wouldn't normally get to see and getting comfortable with something that once scared me. Every dive gives me something new to learn. And yes, I already think about the next one before the previous one is over.

process

how my brain works

chaotic at the start. structured by the end.

I don't always start with a neat plan. Sometimes I start with a question, sometimes with a problem that annoys me, and sometimes with too many ideas. But I do have a way of bringing it all together.

  • the noise01 · everything starts somewhere.
    Questions, ideas, references, random thoughts and a lot of "what if?" I collect them first. I don't want to throw away a good idea just because I haven't figured it out yet.
  • research02 · stop guessing. start asking.
    I look at the problem, the people and what's already out there. Surveys, interviews and research help me understand what actually matters instead of designing around assumptions.
  • structure03 · make the mess make sense.
    Personas, sitemaps, information architecture and user flows. This is where I start connecting the dots. If the structure doesn't work, the pretty screens won't save it.
  • design04 · now make it feel right.
    Wireframes first, then interfaces, interactions and details. I want things to be clear, consistent and comfortable to use. I pay attention to the little things because they add up.
  • build05 · out of figma. into reality.
    HTML, SCSS, JavaScript, React, Vue and GSAP. I enjoy seeing a design become something functional. And when something doesn't work, well, that's another problem to solve.
  • question it again06 · is it actually good?
    I test, adjust, break things and improve them. I'm rarely completely satisfied with the first version. There is usually something I can make clearer, smoother or simpler.
